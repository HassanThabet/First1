<product_requirements>
The مدارس الفجر الجديد الأهلية system requires distinct dashboards for Admin, Chairman, Director, Vice-Principal, Supervisor, Activities, Educational Supervision, and Social Specialist roles, supporting two branches (Boys/Girls) for all except Admin. Core features include username/password authentication without JWT, full Arabic UI with RTL, responsive design, and professional branding. Users must create, view, edit, and delete their reports, which should feature graphical statistics. Data export to Excel and PDF formats is mandatory.

Role-specific requirements: Supervisor for daily reports, Vice-Principal for tasks and merged supervisor reports. Activities for activity reports with multi-select teacher fields. Social Specialist for student case reports including total case counts. Educational Supervision for teacher performance. Quality for academic/program reports. Director views all reports with staff-specific filtering and aggregated statistics. Chairman views Director's summaries, all staff reports with branch/agent-specific filtering, and teacher evaluations. Admin manages accounts (auto-assigning Chairman to 'both' branches), teachers, classes, and subjects. The system needs comprehensive, filterable (daily/weekly/monthly/date range/branch/agent) Excel and PDF exports containing all report details.
